Section 01

Scope of Service & On-Device Processing

Family Intelligence is designed to empower Indian households to organize finances, documents, tasks, and vehicles. Natural language categorizations are performed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (Google LiteRT runtime) without transmitting financial payloads to external AI clouds.

Section 02

Financial Data Isolation & No Cloud Sync

Under our strict architecture directives, financial transactions, account numbers, balances, and recurring ledgers are strictly local to your physical device. Even if cross-device family synchronization is active for chores, occasions, or contacts, financial records are hardcoded to never sync to the cloud.

Section 03

SMS Permissions & User Control

Granting SMS permissions is optional and utilized solely for local parsing of transactional SMS alerts sent by banks and credit cards. You may revoke SMS permissions at any time in Android settings; the application will remain completely functional for manual entries.

Section 04

Household Syncing & Family Sharing

When you pair two devices via a family pairing code, non-financial household data (occasions, contacts, insurance reminders) is synced via Google Firebase Firestore (asia-south1 region). You are responsible for ensuring that you only pair devices with trusted family members.

Section 05

Account Termination & Data Deletion

You may request full account and cloud sync deletion at any time by emailing nalkudilstudio@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all paired cloud records are permanently purged within 30 days in full compliance with Google Play Account Deletion mandates.

Section 06

Disclaimer of Financial Advisory

Calculations, health scores, and projections are deterministic models for budgeting purposes only and do not constitute certified financial or legal counsel.
