Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: July 2026  ·  🏢 Nalkudil Studio  ·  📧 nalkudilstudio@gmail.com

Our commitment: Family Intelligence handles your family's most sensitive data — finances, health appointments, school records. We treat it with the utmost care. Your financial data is processed and stored entirely on your own device, our on-device AI runs fully offline, and nothing is ever sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train any AI model.

Section 01

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account details — name and email via Google Sign-In
  • Financial data — transactions, account balances, credit card details, loan records, recurring items (processed and stored entirely on your device — see Section 4)
  • Family data — occasions, appointments, school records, grocery lists, home planner tasks, insurance, contacts
  • Family structure — member names, roles (SELF / SPOUSE), and relationship to the account holder, including any minors added by a parent
  • Pairing data — a unique family ID generated when two devices are linked

Collected automatically:

  • Android version and device model for Firebase crash reporting only
  • No advertising IDs, no location data, no microphone or camera access

Downloaded to your device (not "collected" — this is app functionality, not your data):

  • An on-device AI model (~2.6GB, downloaded once) used to categorise transactions entirely offline
Section 02

SMS Permission

Family Intelligence requests SMS permission for the SMS Auto-Parse feature, which reads incoming bank and card messages to automatically extract transaction details.

  • SMS reading and AI categorisation happen entirely on your device
  • No SMS content is ever uploaded to our servers
  • Extracted transaction data (amount, merchant, date) is stored locally on your device only — it is never uploaded to Firebase or any server
  • You can decline the SMS permission — all other features continue to work normally with manual entry
  • The SMS permission is never used for any other purpose
Section 03

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Family Intelligence:

  • To sync non-financial family data (members, contacts, insurance, vehicles, occasions, festival budgets, civic bills, and similar) between your paired devices in real time — your financial data is never part of this sync, by design
  • To calculate your monthly dashboard, cashflow projection, financial health score, and loan schedules — entirely on your device
  • To send push notifications for occasion reminders, bill due dates, and a weekly family summary you have opted into
  • To improve stability through anonymised Firebase crash reports
  • If you choose to tap "Report" on a misread SMS, a masked snippet (all amounts, account numbers, and phone numbers scrubbed) is sent to us solely to improve parsing accuracy — this is never automatic and never includes your raw SMS

We never: sell your data · share with advertisers · use your financial content or SMS data to train any AI model

Section 04

Data Storage & Security

Family Intelligence splits your data into three tiers, so it's always clear what happens to what:

TierWhat's in itWhere it lives
Never leaves your deviceAll transactions, account balances, credit card & loan details, subscriptions, recurring payments, raw SMS bodies, the AI model itselfLocal device storage only
Synced with your paired family memberFamily members & contacts, insurance, vehicles, occasions & festival budgets, civic bills, UPI IDs, chit funds, gold schemes, document renewalsGoogle Firebase (Firestore), asia-south1 region (Mumbai, India)
Backed up to your own Google Drive (optional)Nearly everything, including your financial data — restorable if you switch phonesYour private, app-only Drive folder — AES-256 encrypted, not visible to us or anyone else. Up to 4 recent backups kept, auto-rotated
  • Authentication is handled by Google Sign-In — your password is managed by Google, not us
  • Biometric lock (if enabled) is processed entirely on-device using Android's BiometricManager
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict each family's synced data to authenticated members of that family only
  • The on-device AI model runs via Google's LiteRT runtime — no transaction, SMS, or financial content is ever sent to any cloud AI provider for processing

Section 06

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Google Drive backups (if enabled) keep up to 4 recent copies, auto-rotated as new backups are made. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at nalkudilstudio@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days of your request.

Section 07

Children's Data

Family Intelligence is designed for adults managing a household. Where a parent adds a child as a family member — for example, for school fees or an occasion reminder — that data is:

  • Entered and controlled by the parent, not the child; the app is not directed at or used by children independently
  • Accessible only to the parents of that family
  • Never used for tracking, profiling, or advertising of any kind
  • Never shared or sold to any third party
  • Deleted along with the parent's account upon request

In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we treat any data about a minor as requiring the consent and control of their parent or guardian at all times, and we do not process a child's data for any purpose beyond what the parent has directly entered the app to do.

Section 08

Your Rights

Under India's DPDP Act and general good practice, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data, including full account deletion
  • Withdraw consent at any time — for example, by revoking SMS permission or disabling sync — without it affecting the lawfulness of anything already done
  • Port your data via Google Drive backup export
  • Raise a grievance if you're unsatisfied with how we've handled a request

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nalkudilstudio@gmail.com — as a small studio, every request is read and handled personally, and we aim to respond within 30 days.
