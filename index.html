Getting Started

Up & Running in 3 Steps

From download to your first shared transaction — here's how to get set up.

01

📲 Download & Sign In

Family Intelligence uses Google Sign-In for a secure, stable account that survives reinstalls.

  • Download Family Intelligence from the Google Play Store
  • Tap 'Sign in with Google' and select your account
  • Complete the quick onboarding — enter your name and your partner's name
  • Your account is ready — no password to remember
  • Enable biometric lock in Settings for extra security
02

💳 Set Up Your Accounts & Opening Balances

Add your bank accounts, credit cards, and cash wallets so the dashboard reflects your real financial picture.

  • Go to Money → Accounts → Add Account
  • Add each account: Bank / Credit Card / Pay Later / Cash
  • Enter the current balance as the opening balance
  • For credit cards: add billing cycle day and payment due day
  • Transactions you log will update balances from this point forward
03

🔗 Pair with Your Partner

Family Intelligence creates a shared, synced space for two devices — yours and your partner's.

  • One partner opens Settings → Pairing → Generate Code
  • Share the 6-character code with your partner
  • Partner enters the code on their device and confirms
  • Both devices now sync family members, insurance, vehicles, occasions, and more — your transactions, balances, and accounts always stay private to each device
  • Each person's shared data appears correctly attributed to them

FAQ

Common Questions

Is my financial data secure?
Yes — and more than that, your transactions, account balances, and card details never leave your device at all. They're not uploaded to Firebase or any server, and aren't part of family sync. Only non-financial family data (members, insurance, vehicles, occasions) syncs between paired devices, over Google Firebase (asia-south1 region, ISO 27001 / SOC 1/2/3 / GDPR certified). We use Google Sign-In — no passwords are stored by us. Nothing is ever sold or shared with advertisers.
Does the on-device AI send my data anywhere?
No. The AI model that categorises your transactions runs entirely on your phone using Google's LiteRT runtime — it's downloaded once (~2.6GB) and never contacts a server to process your SMS or transactions. The only exception is fully optional: if you manually tap "Report" on a misread SMS, a masked snippet (amounts, account numbers, and phone numbers scrubbed) is sent to us to improve accuracy — never automatic.
How does SMS auto-parsing work?
Family Intelligence reads incoming bank and card SMS messages on-device to extract transaction details. No message content is uploaded. You can decline the SMS permission — all other features continue to work normally with manual entry.
Can I use Family Intelligence without pairing?
Yes — all modules work for a single user. Pairing enables real-time sync of non-financial family data between two devices. You can pair anytime after initial setup.
What happens if I reinstall the app?
Because Family Intelligence uses Google Sign-In, your UID is stable across reinstalls, and your synced family data (members, insurance, vehicles, occasions) restores automatically from Firebase. Your financial data — transactions, accounts, balances — restores only from your own encrypted Google Drive backup, so we'd recommend enabling backup in Settings before you ever need it.
How does credit card balance work?
When you log a CC transaction, your bank balance is not affected — only the card's outstanding balance increases. When you pay the bill (Pay CC Bill), both the bank balance and the outstanding balance decrease. This mirrors real-world deferred billing.
